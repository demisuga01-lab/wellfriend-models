# OCR benchmark boundary

MP5 provides text-only CER and WER metric infrastructure, not OCR inference. Candidate systems such as PaddleOCR, Tesseract, platform OCR, and future custom OCR may be compared only after their code, weights, languages, datasets, and licenses are audited.

ML Kit can be a comparative platform integration later; it is not a bundled Python training dependency. Evaluation records must name the text normalization, language/script, corpus license, model revision, runtime/device, and failures. No OCR model or corpus is redistributed here.

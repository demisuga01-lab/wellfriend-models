# Training, evaluation, and export

MP1 reserves reproducible entrypoints for document segmentation, corner regression, quality prediction, restoration, cleanup masks, and OCR benchmarking. Evaluation records IoU, corner error, CER, WER, SSIM, MS-SSIM, PSNR, geometric distortion, latency, memory, model size, and FLOPs/MACs. Export must produce ONNX plus the complete validated artifact contract.
